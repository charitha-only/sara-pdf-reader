# Sara PDF Reader v1.06

Android build 11. Android 8.0 or later.

- First reader launch after install/update opens Camera and Photos permission setup and requests access through Android system dialogs. Existing users are included once in this migration.
- Settings & privacy > Permissions shows current Camera and Photos access, offers request/reselection buttons and opens Android app settings after persistent denials. Status refreshes after returning from Settings.
- Android 13+ uses Photos access; Android 14+ distinguishes all photos, selected photos only, and denied. Android 8–12 uses the older media/storage read permission. No microphone, video, location or all-files-management permission is requested.
- Camera capture requests Camera permission first; denial returns guidance instead of launching a capture that Android would reject.
- Document opening and external saving continue to use user-selected Android file/destination grants. App-private data needs no storage permission. Photo permission does not grant arbitrary access to other apps or every phone file. Reading selected documents remains available after photo denial.

Android controls whether a permission dialog appears. Already granted permissions are not prompted again; repeated denials may require granting access in Android app settings. Access can be revoked later. Physical-phone validation remains pending.

Verification: 44 app tests passed; analysis clean. Signed API36 emulator fresh-install test showed the Android Camera system dialog immediately on launch, before Get Started, followed by the Photos system dialog. Android package state confirmed Camera/Photos grants, and camera launch after granting access succeeded. Partial-access display and denial-safe capture are covered by widget tests.
