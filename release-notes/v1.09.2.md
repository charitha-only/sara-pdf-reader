# Sara PDF Reader v1.09.2

Android build 16. Offline camera scan improvements.

- Retake page replaces only the selected scan after a successful capture and cleanup. Cancelling or failing leaves the earlier page intact, and other pages/order stay unchanged. Retakes also work at the 20-page limit; the draft byte budget still applies.
- Image cleanup adds Clean colour and Black & white presets and a Show original comparison toggle. Export includes applied adjustments; Gallery files remain unchanged. Black & white intentionally removes stamp/photo colours; choose Clean colour to retain them.
- Shadow correction uses a separable local paper-background estimate, reducing envelope-estimation work, bilinear lighting interpolation to avoid block boundaries, and a smooth dark-ink protection curve to avoid abrupt tone jumps.
- Capture guidance reminds you to hold steady, include the page and avoid glare. Reviewed crop/perspective correction, saved drafts, multi-page PDF, PNG/JPEG, sharing and printing remain available.

Processing stays entirely on-device, with no new dependencies/network permission/model downloads. Scan images remain limited to 2400 pixels on the longest edge and JPEG quality 92. Review results: severe shadows/glare/blur cannot always be recovered. Physical phone/printer verification remains pending.

Validation: 63 app tests passed; source analysis clean. New tests verify colour/chroma behaviour, smooth dark-ink tone transitions, cancelled retakes and replacing only the selected page. The signed API36 emulator release opened both cleanup presets and original comparison, applied cleanup, retained both pages exactly after cancelling native Camera retake, and replaced only page 2 after a successful native capture while retaining page 1 and saving auto-clean state. Current-process logs contained no fatal/unhandled/overflow errors. All three APKs passed signing/version/ABI/offline-permission/16 KB ZIP-alignment and APK security checks. Dependency versions are unchanged.
